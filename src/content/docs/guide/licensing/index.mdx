<p class="lead">What a license lets people do with your code, and which one to pick, apply and live with.</p>

- **For:** anyone who publishes code, and anyone whose company asks "can we use this?".
- **You'll need:** a project, or a dependency you're wondering about.
- **Skip if:** your project has a license you chose on purpose, and you know what it asks of its users.

> [!NOTE] We're not lawyers
> This module explains how open-source licenses work in practice, with links to their texts. It isn't legal advice: when real money or a real dispute is involved, ask someone whose job it is.
