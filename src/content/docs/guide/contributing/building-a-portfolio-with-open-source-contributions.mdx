<p class="lead">Open-source contributions can strengthen your resume or portfolio. They show your skills, your expertise and your commitment to the software development community. This chapter covers how contributing helps your portfolio, and how to show your contributions.</p>

## How Contributing to Open-Source Projects Can Enhance Your Portfolio

- <strong>Demonstrating your skills</strong>: Open-source contributions demonstrate your coding skills, problem-solving
  abilities, and creativity. They are evidence of your capabilities and can set you apart from other job candidates.
- <strong>Gaining experience</strong>: Contributing gives you valuable experience working on a team, collaborating with
  others, and contributing to real-world software development projects.
- <strong>Building a reputation</strong>: Consistent contributions help build your reputation as a skilled and reliable
  developer. That makes you more desirable to potential employers or clients.
- <strong>Showing your interests</strong>: Contributing to projects related to your interests shows your passion for
  specific technologies or domains. It makes you more attractive to companies or clients that work in those areas.

## How to Showcase Your Open-Source Contributions

Once you've made contributions to open-source projects, show them off in these places:

- <strong>On your Git repositories profile</strong>: Your GitHub profile, for example, is a great place to show your
  open-source contributions. You can include links to your contributions, descriptions of your work, and any relevant
  feedback you've received.
- <strong>On your website</strong>: A portfolio website that includes your open-source contributions can be an effective
  way to show your skills and expertise. You can include links to your contributions, descriptions of your work, and any
  relevant feedback you've received.
- <strong>On your social media</strong>: Social media platforms such as Bluesky, Mastodon and X can be an effective way
  to show your open-source contributions. You can share links to your contributions, and include descriptions of your
  work and any relevant feedback you've received.
- <strong>On your LinkedIn profile</strong>: LinkedIn is a professional networking site widely used by recruiters and
  employers. Adding your open-source contributions to your LinkedIn profile can be an effective way to show your skills
  and experience to potential employers or clients.
- <strong>In job applications</strong>: When applying for jobs, highlight your open-source contributions on your resume
  or cover letter. This can demonstrate your skills, experience, and commitment to the software development community.
- <strong>In interviews</strong>: If you're invited for an interview, highlight your open-source contributions and
  describe how they show your skills and experience.
