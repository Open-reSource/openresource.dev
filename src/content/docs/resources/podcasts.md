Podcasts about open source, with one episode to start with for each. Every show below published an episode in September or October 2026; we checked each feed on 8 October 2026.

## Shows about the people who maintain open source

- [Sustain](https://podcast.sustainoss.org/) by SustainOSS · weekly · [RSS](https://podcast.sustainoss.org/rss) – Conversations with practitioners, funders and maintainers about the health, culture and sustainability of the open source ecosystem beyond licenses. **Start with:** [Courtney Miller on maintainer burnout and software abandonment](https://podcast.sustainoss.org/289) (episode 289, June 2026).
- [Coffee & Open Source](https://coffeeandopensource.com) by Isaac Levin · weekly · [RSS](https://isaacl.dev/podcast-rss) – Interviews with notable folks in the <abbr title="Open-Source Software">OSS</abbr> community, many from the .NET world. **Start with:** [Kohsuke Kawaguchi](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/coffeandopensource/episodes/Kohsuke-Kawaguchi-e36pa8o), creator of Jenkins (August 2025).
- [The Changelog](https://changelog.com/podcast) · weekly · [RSS](https://changelog.com/podcast/feed) – Software development and open source news and interviews. **Start with:** [Curl is a full-time job (and turns 23)](https://changelog.com/podcast/436) (April 2021), Daniel Stenberg on what running a widely used project costs.

## Shows about security and money

- [Open Source Security Podcast](https://opensourcesecurity.io) by Kurt Seifried and Josh Bressers · weekly · [RSS](https://opensourcesecurity.io/feed.xml) – Vulnerabilities, supply chain attacks and the policy around them. **Start with:** [Hobbyist Maintainers with Thomas DePierre](https://opensourcesecurity.io/2025/2025-06-hobbyist-thomas-depierre/) (June 2025).
- [Open Source Startup Podcast](https://oss-startup-podcast.launchnotes.io) · roughly every two weeks · [RSS](https://anchor.fm/s/3eab794c/podcast/rss) – Founders and investors building companies on open source projects. **Start with:** [E185: The challenges with monetizing open source](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/ossstartuppodcast/episodes/E185-The-Challenges-with-Monetizing-Open-Source-with-the-Creator-of-Rich--Textual-e3agqef) (November 2025).

## Shows about the tools

- [FLOSS Weekly](https://hackaday.com/floss-weekly/) by Hackaday, hosted by Jonathan Bennett · weekly · [RSS](https://feeds.libsyn.com/499093/rss) – One free and open source project per episode, with the person behind it. The show moved to Hackaday after the old TWiT feed stopped in January 2024. **Start with:** [Episode 808, Curl: Gotta Download 'em All](https://hackaday.com/2024/11/06/floss-weekly-episode-808-curl-gotta-download-em-all/) (November 2024).

## Talks

Not podcasts, but worth the same hour:

- [curl – a hobby project with a billion users](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lq2ksox21Eg) by Daniel Stenberg (Google Tech Talk Meetup, August 2015) – how one maintainer grew a hobby project into software installed on billions of devices.
- [The real costs of Open Source Sustainability](https://archive.org/details/cern2019-osssustainability) (CERN, October 2019) – the case from _Roads and Bridges_, Nadia Eghbal's report on how few people maintain the software the internet runs on.
- [Working in public: Nadia Eghbal in conversation](https://leaddev.com/software-quality/working-public-nadia-eghbal-conversation) (LeadDev, November 2020) – what open source maintenance looks like when a few people serve many. Free LeadDev account required.
- [How to Secure the Software Supply Chain](https://infocondb.org/con/dutch-hacker-camps/may-contain-hackers-mch2022/how-to-secure-the-software-supply-chain) by Feross Aboukhadijeh (May Contain Hackers, 2022) – malware found in npm packages, and what to check before you add a dependency.
