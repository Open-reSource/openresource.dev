Ten licenses that cover most of the open source you will meet, with the identifier to put in your `license` field. Summaries follow [choosealicense.com](https://choosealicense.com/appendix/) and were checked in October 2026. This is a reference, not legal advice.

## What the columns mean

- **License** is the [SPDX](https://spdx.org/licenses/) identifier: the string package managers and compliance tools read. Copy it as written.
- **The user must** is what the license asks of anyone who uses, changes or shares your code.
- **Patent grant** says whether contributors license the patents their contribution needs. Apache-2.0 ends that grant for anyone who sues over patents in the project.
- **Copyleft covers** is how far "changes must come back" reaches: nothing, the modified files, the library or the whole work.
- **OSI-approved** links to the [Open Source Initiative's](https://opensource.org/licenses) page for the license. CC0-1.0 is not approved by the OSI, and says so itself: it waives rights, it does not license the code.

Every one of them disclaims warranty and liability: the code comes as is.
