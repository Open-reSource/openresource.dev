<p class="lead">Bluesky released starter packs on June 26, 2024. They are personalized invites that allow users to bring people directly into your slice of Bluesky!</p>

[Bluesky](https://bsky.social), the decentralized social media platform, [introduced starter packs on June 26, 2024](https://bsky.social/about/blog/06-26-2024-starter-packs), aiming to personalize user experiences and make it easier for communities to grow. They let users bring friends directly into their customized Bluesky environment.

## What's in a Bluesky Starter Pack?

A Bluesky starter pack can include the following:

- **Custom feeds**: On Bluesky, you can set any algorithm or topic as your home timeline.
- **Recommended follows**: Add your favorite accounts and encourage new users to follow them.

As of September 2026:

- **Starter packs show up in search**, in the "Starter Packs" tab. At launch they didn't, and a link or an embed preview was the only way in.
- **Starter packs can recommend up to 500 people and up to 3 custom feeds**. The limit was 150 people at launch. New users will automatically have Following and Discover pinned.
