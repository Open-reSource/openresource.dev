<p class="lead">Julien Déramond writes Open \{re}Source with a few contributors, on Astro, hosted on Vercel. Drafts are produced with Claude models, and a human reads, edits and verifies every published word.</p>

## Julien writes it, contributors fix it

Open \{re}Source is maintained by [Julien Déramond](/about-author/) since 2023. Other people improve it through pull requests: the [contributors graph](https://github.com/Open-reSource/openresource.dev/graphs/contributors) on GitHub is the up-to-date list, so this page does not keep a copy that could go stale.

[![Contributors to Open {re}Source](https://contrib.rocks/image?repo=Open-reSource/openresource.dev)](https://github.com/Open-reSource/openresource.dev/graphs/contributors)

## The site is a static Astro build with a shared theme

- [Astro](https://astro.build) with MDX for the pages, the guide, the articles and the resources.
- The theme and layout come from [`@deramond.dev/astro`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@deramond.dev/astro).
- Hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com), with Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies (see the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/)).
- Internal links are checked with [lychee](https://github.com/lycheeverse/lychee) and spelling with [cspell](https://cspell.org), on every pull request.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Open-reSource/openresource.dev).

## AI drafts, a human decides

Most pages start as a draft written with Claude models, which follow a written style guide for voice, structure and sources. A human then reads every published word, edits it and rewrites what does not sound right. Pull requests are reviewed by Julien before they are merged.

AI does not produce the evidence. Screenshots are taken for real in the product they show. Numbers are checked against their source and dated. A claim we cannot verify does not make it into the page.

Behind this, a private planning backlog turns ideas into issues, and issues into pull requests.
